  attack/release on one sample at a time.
- A separate `master_out_usb()` for the USB output path.
- In `mix_block()` (end of file), a sample-by-sample loop that calls
  `master_out(&l, &r)` for every sample.

**What to put in its place** (copy from the upstream
`/Users/ischwartz/sloop-fm1/firmware/src/fx.c`):

1. Replace `lim_env` with the `lim_t` struct and two instances `lim_dac` /
   `lim_usb` (upstream lines 63–82). Copy the struct definition and the two
   static instances verbatim.

2. Copy the `lim_block()` function verbatim (upstream lines 76–116). It takes
