Custom firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

Six tracks, six synth engines — DX7 FM, analogue, Phase Distortion, formant voice, lo-fi chip, plucked strings — a drum machine with circuit-modelled 808 & 909, your own samples, a chord sequencer with 14-mode chord follow, a 32-step global chord progression, song mode you play with your hands, evolving generative patterns and fills, USB audio, MIDI keyboard and clock. No factory patterns, no presets to scroll through: everything you hear, you play.
